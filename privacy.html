Kept Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

Kept does not collect, send, sell, or analyze your data. All prompts, folders, and settings are stored locally in your browser. They never leave your computer unless you export a backup file yourself.

What Kept stores

The prompts, folders, and settings you create are saved in your browser using Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local). This data stays on your computer. It is not sent to any server and is not synced to other devices.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or servers.
  • No advertising, usage tracking, or in-app analytics.
  • No network requests are made by the extension.
  • No access to the websites you visit. The extension does not read or change web pages.

Permissions

  • storage – to save your prompts locally in your browser.
  • sidePanel – to show the extension in Chrome's side panel.

The Copy button writes the prompt you click to your clipboard only when you click it.

Backups and imports

Exporting creates a file that is saved on your computer by your browser. Importing reads a file you choose from your computer. Neither action sends data anywhere. You are responsible for the files you export.

Deleting your data

Deleted prompts move to the Trash and can be restored until you empty the Trash or delete them permanently. Uninstalling the extension removes all data it stored in your browser, so export a backup first if you want to keep your prompts.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
